# Shared design details

The reference system uses the owner's green, cream, lilac, Autour One headings and DM Sans support. This pass gives small details defined roles rather than adding unrelated decoration.

- **Section edges:** one 144px horizontal scallop rhythm. Header: cream, downward-facing, 48px deep. Footer: green, upward-facing, 48px deep. The header and footer share the same rounded proportions; the lighter cream header repeats the green footer without a thin divider.
- **Small circles:** active-page indicator is a four-dot placement measured from the original logo, scaled with the rendered logo, echoing the small circular details in the actual logo. Social controls remain circular and the confirmation check remains a filled green circle with negative-space check. Active state also retains aria-current; keyboard focus is a separate visible ring.
- **Control outlines:** shared 2px green stroke, rounded pill shape and 52px height for email controls. Cream outlines reverse the same treatment on green. Noninteractive section edges use shape and color rather than arbitrary hairlines.
- **Hierarchy:** broad shape at page boundaries, small functional marks near navigation and controls. Avoid scallops inside content panels or repeated decorative stamps. Autour One headlines lead; eyebrow labels remain small uppercase DM Sans 600; regular body copy remains subordinate.
- **Proximity and alignment:** email field and button share a bottom edge even when a field label is visible. The check stays with the confirmation heading. Shared 1280px outer alignment governs the navigation, maker layout and footer; the newsletter uses a narrower reading/form measure.
- **Interaction:** primary buttons darken within the original green palette. Focus rings remain strong and visible, and hover effects do not move the layout. No decorative animation is added.

The header and footer are the same across all pages. On cream-to-cream pages the cream header edge blends into its surrounding surface instead of inventing a new separator. Body copy, navigation destinations, subscription behavior and brand artwork are unchanged by this pass.
