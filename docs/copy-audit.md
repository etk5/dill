# Website copy audit

Scope: all three HTML pages, shared navigation/footer, page titles/descriptions, accessible labels and site-owned form feedback. Klaviyo-managed content is listed separately. This review uses the owner's instructions, known shop/profile links and existing uploaded brand materials; it does not claim an independent review of current Etsy listings or the Klaviyo account.

## Voice and factual basis
Use “Dillweed & Dirt,” “pottery,” “functional ceramics,” “newsletter” and “shop.” Do not invent a studio identity, membership program, personal biography, location, production schedule or behind-the-scenes email promise. Avoid making the website sound as if the owner has supplied a story that has not actually been provided. Keep link labels aligned with their destination and use one clear primary purpose per page. Warmth should come from plain, specific language rather than repeated “little,” vague rituals or unsupported lore.

## Klaviyo-owned copy: requires account publication

The website mounts form VphHay; it does not own the form's text or account/list settings. The owner's supplied audit reports Email List uses single opt-in. Recommended account copy:

- Field label: “Email address”.
- Placeholder: “Your email address”.
- Button: “Subscribe”.
- Initial heading: omit it, because the website supplies the headline and description.
- Success heading: “You’re on the list!”
- Success body: “Thanks for joining Dillweed & Dirt.”
- Remove “Check your email for a confirmation message” if the list remains single opt-in.

The site's styling adapter supports the recommended success heading and does not overwrite provider consent, validation or success content. Publish text changes in Klaviyo. Live list settings, form delivery and automatic confirmation content were not independently verified in this audit. Preserve accurate consent/privacy and unsubscribe information; do not make a promise about email frequency or exclusivity without an owner decision.

## Remaining content need
The About page is intentionally brief. A detailed maker biography needs the owner's actual words or facts; do not fill the gap with invented narrative. Product descriptions, prices and care claims remain in Etsy and were not audited here. Historical research/design documents may contain earlier proposals; they are not current customer-facing copy.
